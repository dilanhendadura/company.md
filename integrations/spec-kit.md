# Spec Kit adapter

Before creating or revising `spec.md`, select the narrowest Company.md context profile that can constrain the feature. Use `core` for internal architecture, `customer` for problem definition, `commercial` when offer boundaries matter, and `visual` only when the feature includes a designed surface.

Record Company.md source paths and unresolved facts in the spec's source section. Do not copy the pack into `constitution.md`: the constitution governs how work is performed; Company.md governs durable business truth. Plans and tasks must preserve claims to avoid, customer exclusions, classification, and human approval boundaries from the selected context.
