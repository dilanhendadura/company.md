# llms.txt export boundary

`llms.txt` is a public discovery index, not Company.md's source of truth. An organization may link from `llms.txt` to a separately reviewed public Company.md export only when every included statement and source is approved for public disclosure.

Never expose internal, confidential, or restricted packs through `llms.txt`. Public exports must be derived, reviewable artifacts and must not replace the governed source pack.
