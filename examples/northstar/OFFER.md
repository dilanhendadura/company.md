# Northstar Cloud offer context

## Packages

**Foundation** establishes one production workflow and its controls. **Scale** adds multiple workflows, reusable components, and operating analytics. **Enterprise** adds advanced environment, identity, support, and governance requirements. Package availability and naming must be confirmed against the current quoting system before use in a proposal.

## Deliverables

Every initial engagement includes process discovery, a boundary map, configured workflow, approval rules, test scenarios, administrator enablement, and an operating review. Source-system remediation, bespoke model training, and customer policy creation are not included unless separately scoped.

## Pricing Logic

Pricing reflects workflow complexity, controlled environments, connector and execution volume, support level, and implementation effort. Agents may explain these drivers but must retrieve current numbers from the quoting system and route non-standard terms to Revenue Operations.

## Proof

Approved proof must identify the customer segment, measured baseline, measurement window, and exact product scope. An anecdote can illustrate a workflow but cannot substantiate a performance claim. Public use requires separate customer and Legal approval.

## Promises

We promise a defined implementation boundary, visible agent activity, named human approval points, documented tests, and an operating handoff. Discovery is required before a final implementation scope {claim:offer.discovery-required}.

## Claims to Avoid

Never claim “zero risk,” “fully autonomous,” “error-free,” “compliant by default,” “replaces your compliance team,” or a guaranteed financial or cycle-time outcome {claim:offer.no-outcome-guarantee}. Do not imply certification, residency, or connector availability without checking the relevant approved source.

## Good-Fit Customers

A good-fit customer has a named workflow and owner, accessible subject-matter experts, an executive sponsor, a control owner, a measurable baseline, and capacity to integrate the required systems. The first workflow is important enough to matter and bounded enough to test.

## Commercial Guardrails

Only the quoting system supplies current prices. Legal approves public claims, non-standard liability, data-use language, and customer-logo use. Security Architecture approves unsupported data-residency or integration assertions. An agent may draft but may not commit the company to a price, term, result, or delivery date.

## Evidence and Open Questions

The three package names are approved for internal planning but may differ by market. Revenue Operations owns quarterly reconciliation with the quoting catalog. Product Marketing owns proof expiry and approved public wording.
