# Community standards

We want Company.md to be a useful, rigorous, and welcoming open-source project.

Be respectful, critique ideas with evidence, assume good intent while taking impact seriously, and make space for people with different disciplines and levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the hosting platform. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
