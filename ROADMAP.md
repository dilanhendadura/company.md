# Roadmap

Company.md will earn adoption by making company-aware agent work measurably more reliable. Dates are intentionally absent; each milestone closes evidence gates rather than shipping on a marketing deadline.

## Shipped in 0.3

- Public Codex marketplace and plugin installation from the GitHub repository.
- Isolated `$company` routing test against an active enterprise-mode pack.
- Clean-package workflows on Linux, macOS, and Windows.
- Required coverage, dependency review, production audit, and CodeQL gates on `main`.

## Now — make the first workflow undeniable

- Publish the `company.md` npm package with trusted publishing and provenance.
- Complete the Meridian sales-deck evaluation with a real `.pptx`, rendered-slide inspection, and traceability receipt.
- Add at least three sanitized adopter evaluations from different organization shapes.
- Measure time-to-first-valid-pack and reduce avoidable authoring friction.
- Publish a short, reproducible video showing setup, `$company`, artifact creation, and the receipt.

## Next — prove interoperability

- Add tested routing guides for additional coding agents without duplicating business truth.
- Define portable overlay fixtures for business unit, product, region, and locale scope.
- Add claim-expiry and evidence-refresh workflows suitable for CI.
- Expand artifact evaluations beyond decks to proposals, landing pages, support replies, and product copy.
- Add machine-readable conformance fixtures for third-party implementations.

## Toward 1.0 — stabilize the standard

Version 1.0 requires:

1. at least five real pilots across three organization shapes;
2. no unresolved high-severity security findings;
3. a documented migration policy tested against all published fixtures;
4. stable rule identifiers and context receipt schema;
5. validated behavior on supported Node.js versions and operating systems;
6. at least two independent maintainers or an explicit single-maintainer continuity plan;
7. evidence that the four-file minimum remains usable by non-technical owners.

## Explicit non-goals

- Replacing a CRM, CMS, DAM, policy engine, or access-control system.
- Storing secrets, raw personal data, or an unrestricted enterprise knowledge base.
- Creating a universal truth score for generated content.
- Automatically activating agent-authored company claims.
- Copying every vendor's instruction format into the core standard.

Propose changes through the [format proposal template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/format_proposal.yml). A roadmap item is not a commitment until its implementation and evidence are merged.
