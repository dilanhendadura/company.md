# Sales deck evaluation scenario

Invoke `$company` from the Company.md repository root.

Create an 8-slide PowerPoint sales deck for Meridian Mutual using the approved Northstar Cloud company and design context.

Audience: Chief Operating Officer and Head of Compliance.

Purpose: secure agreement to a 90-minute workflow discovery session, not close a commercial agreement.

Known client facts supplied for this scenario:

- Meridian Mutual is a fictional EU insurer with approximately 1,400 employees.
- Its claims-exception workflow crosses Operations, Compliance, and IT.
- Evidence is currently assembled from email, shared drives, and spreadsheets.
- A recent internal audit identified inconsistent approval records.
- Humans must retain the final approval decision.

Requirements:

- Write the deck in English.
- Use the linked `DESIGN.md`.
- Do not include pricing, unsupported performance statistics, customer logos, or invented integrations.
- End with a concrete discovery-session agenda.
- Produce a `.pptx` and its Company.md context receipt.
